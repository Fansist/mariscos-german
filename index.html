Mariscos German

Mariscos from San Felipe, Baja California, at 4724 Federal Blvd. Tacos, tostadas, cocteles, oysters and pata de mula.

Every day, 9 AM to 7 PM. Call before you drive over.

Call (619) 540-7822Directions (opens in a new tab)

Consomé while you wait, on the house.

Customers are handed a cup of hot seafood broth while their order is made. There is no charge for it.

Consomé is also on the board, under Caldos.

The menu board

Known for crispy fish and shrimp tacos, marlin tacos, ceviche, aguachile, oysters, and pata de mula clams.

Family-run since 2000.

Mariscos German comes from San Felipe, Baja California, and was established in 2000. It is credited as the first seafood lonchera, a seafood truck, in San Diego.

Food blog Kirbie's Cravings has written about it. The photos on this page are from 2015, at a Mariscos German truck.
